## About This Guide

Every Azure Frontier Labs workshop runs inside an **isolated, pre-provisioned cloud environment** — no Azure subscription setup required. Before you can start any workshop (AI Lab, Analytics, Big Data, and more), you need to claim your environment.

This guide walks you through the two things every participant does on Day 0:

1. Sign in to the Azure Frontier Labs portal with your lab code and pick up your temporary credentials.

### Prerequisites

- An active Azure Frontier Labs **lab code** (provided by your instructor or via your registration email)
- A web browser (Chrome or Edge recommended)

---

## Step 1 — Log in to the Azure Frontier Labs Portal and obtain lab credentials

Navigate to [azurefrontierlabs.com/join](https://azurefrontierlabs.com/join), paste the **lab code** and your **work email** address, and click **Next**.

![Azure Frontier Labs — signin](/assets/img/workshops/fabric-quickstart/01-labs-dashboard.png)

Review and accept the terms and conditions, then click **Join Lab**.

![Azure Frontier Labs dashboard — accept TOS](/assets/img/workshops/fabric-quickstart/01-terms.png)

You will receive a temporary environment **username** and **password** for your dedicated lab environment. Keep this tab open — you will use these credentials in the next step.

![Azure Frontier Labs dashboard — obtain credentials](/assets/img/posts/claim-azure-environment/SCR-20260602-ipdr.png)

---

## Step 2 — Sign-in to Azure Portal

Click to **Open Azure Portal** and sign in using your temporary credentials.

![Azure Frontier Labs portal signin](/assets/img/posts/claim-azure-environment/SCR-20260602-iquw.png)

![Azure Frontier Labs portal signin](/assets/img/posts/claim-azure-environment/SCR-20260602-irfz.png)

![Azure Frontier Labs portal signin](/assets/img/posts/claim-azure-environment/SCR-20260602-irmg.png)

---

## You're In - locate your resource group

Upon successful signin to Azure Portal navigate to [**Resource Groups**](https://portal.azure.com/#servicemenu/Microsoft_Azure_Resources/ResourceManager/resourcegroups) to see what services are provisioned for you on this lab.

![Azure Frontier Labs portal signin](/assets/img/posts/claim-azure-environment/SCR-20260602-irwz.png)
